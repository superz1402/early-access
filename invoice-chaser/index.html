What PaidChase will do

  • Chase every unpaid invoice on a set cadence — a friendly nudge, then a firmer reminder, then a final notice — written to sound like you, not a collections robot.

  • Work with the invoices you already send (QuickBooks, Wave, Stripe, email/PDF) — no new accounting app to move into, and a weekly report shows exactly who was chased and what came back.

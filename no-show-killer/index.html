What SlotGuard will do

  • Reminders that require a reply. Clients confirm with one tap; no reply means the slot is flagged while there's still time to refill it — not after it goes empty.
  • One-tap reschedule in every reminder. A client who would have ghosted instead moves their booking, and the freed slot can be offered to your waitlist.
  • Optional deposit link for high-value slots. In one r/smallbusiness thread, an operator reported a $25 deposit on a $150 service cut no-shows from roughly 30% to under 10% within two months.
